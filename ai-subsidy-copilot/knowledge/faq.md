# AI Subsidy Program 2026 FAQ

## Is this a real government subsidy?

No. AI Subsidy Copilot is a fictional demonstration. It is not an official government service and no real money, identity verification, or vendor account connection is used.

## Which services are eligible?

ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, and Notion AI are the only automatically eligible products in the current demo policy. See Article 5 of AI Subsidy Program 2026.

## How much can I receive?

Up to NT$600 for one eligible subscription per calendar month. The amount is the lower of the eligible cost in TWD and NT$600. Foreign currency conversion uses clearly labeled static demo rates.

## Can I submit before completing safety training?

No. You may prepare the rest of the application and see a provisional eligibility result, but Article 9 requires all four modules before final submission.

## What happens if the receipt is unclear?

The service should not guess. Low-confidence, incomplete, inconsistent, or suspicious evidence goes to a human reviewer. OCR imperfection alone is not an automatic rejection.

## Can the chatbot approve me?

No. The chatbot can explain retrieved policy and rule results. Deterministic application services apply the rules, reviewers handle exceptions, and the mock treasury validates approval before payment.

## How do I track the result?

Use the public application ID, such as `AI-2026-000183`, on the tracking page. The timeline is built from persisted application and audit events.

## What if the policy does not answer my question?

The assistant should state that the answer cannot be established from the current demo policy. It must not invent a rule or source.
