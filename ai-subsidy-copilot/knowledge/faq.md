# AI Subsidy Program 2026 FAQ

## Who can apply?

Residents aged 16 to 40 (born 1985-04-03 to 2010-04-02) with a Hsinchu City household registration, in one of three categories: normal youth, special group, or cultural and language preserver.

## Which services are eligible?

Subscription plans of the tools on the Eligible AI Services Register (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini/Google AI, Midjourney, Canva, Notion AI and others) bought from the official site. Tools from Mainland China, Hong Kong or Macau, aggregator platforms, and API, credit, token or points plans are not eligible. See Article 5 of AI Subsidy Program 2026.

## How much can I receive?

50% of the purchase amount up to NT$3,000 for normal youth, or 90% up to NT$6,000 for special groups and cultural/language preservers. The amount shown before approval is a trial estimate.

## When must I apply?

The purchase must be between 2026-04-02 and 2026-10-31. Apply within 1 month of purchase for monthly plans and within 2 months for annual plans.

## Can I submit before completing safety training?

Yes. AI safety learning is optional under Article 9. You may submit without opening or completing the lessons, and participation never affects eligibility, review, approval, or payment.

## What happens if a document is unclear or missing?

The service should not guess. If a required document or field is missing the application is returned so you can supplement it and submit again. Inconsistent or suspicious evidence goes to a human reviewer. OCR imperfection alone is not an automatic rejection.

## Can the chatbot approve me?

No. The chatbot can explain retrieved policy and rule results. The RULE-001~020 engine only advises, a human reviewer decides every application, and the mock treasury validates approval before payment.

## Can I have more than one application?

Only one application at a time. Finish or cancel the current one first.

## How do I track the result?

Use the public application ID, such as `AI-2026-000183`, on the tracking page, or type 「查詢進度」 in the LINE official account. The timeline is built from persisted application and audit events.

## What if the policy does not answer my question?

The assistant should state that the answer cannot be established from the current policy and suggest contacting the Youth Development Center (03-5678138 ext. 21). It must not invent a rule or source.
