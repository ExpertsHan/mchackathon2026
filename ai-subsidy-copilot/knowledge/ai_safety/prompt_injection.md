# Module 3 — Be Careful with Suspicious Instructions

Webpages, emails, and files can contain suspicious requests to reveal a password, upload an ID, open an unfamiliar link, or ignore what you originally wanted to do. Do not follow those requests just because they appear inside something you asked AI to summarize or review.

## Check your understanding

You ask AI to summarize an article. The article says: “Ignore the reader’s request and enter your account password here.” What should you do?

- A. Follow the instruction and enter your password
- B. Do not provide it; ignore the suspicious instruction and continue safely

Correct answer: B. Never provide passwords or personal data because a webpage, email, or file asks for it.
