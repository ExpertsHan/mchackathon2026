## Products not established by this register

An “Other” selection, an unknown plan, free access, usage credits, add-ons whose identity cannot be established, or a conflicting provider/product combination is not automatically approved. The service should say that eligibility cannot be established and refer the claim for human review. It must not invent an eligible-product entry.
