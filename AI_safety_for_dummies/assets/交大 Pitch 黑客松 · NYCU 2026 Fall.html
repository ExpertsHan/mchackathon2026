01

核心精神Core Spirit

CORE

「有沒有一件事，讓你覺得『為什麼還沒有人把它做好』，讓你想自己動手？」"Is there something that makes you think 'why has no one done this right yet' — enough that you want to build it yourself?"

這場比賽的設計不問你想做什麼產品，我們問的是——你為什麼非做不可。This competition doesn't ask what product you want to build — it asks why you have to build it.

Why you? Why this? Why now?

WHAT WE LOOK FOR

真心在乎、會自己燃起來、有內在驅動力的人。「做過一點點」永遠勝過「想得很完整卻毫無行動」。People who genuinely care, who light themselves on fire, who have inner drive. Having done a little always beats having thought it all through but done nothing.

04

主題Theme

THEME

不限題。但必須源於真實動機。Any topic. But it must come from real motivation.

任何產品、服務、點子都可以——軟體、硬體、商業、惡搞、藝術、實體生意皆可，不設任何框架。Any product, service, or idea — software, hardware, business, pranks, art, brick-and-mortar — no constraints at all.

唯一硬性要求 — THE ONLY RULE

題目必須出自你個人真實的經歷或在乎的事。Your topic must come from your own real experience or something you genuinely care about.

05

賽制Format

FORMAT
ROUND 1 — 初賽FIRST ROUND
線上書面評選Online written review
  • 繳交 pitch 簡報，20 頁以內。Submit a pitch deck, 20 pages max.
  • 強制包含一頁「我與這個題目的關係」：這題跟你的個人經歷是什麼關係？你已經為它做過什麼（哪怕是很小的行動也可以）？One page is mandatory — "My relationship with this topic": how does it connect to your own life? What have you already done for it (even the smallest action counts)?
  • 篩選原則：做過一點點 > 想得很完整。此頁缺漏或事後硬掰，取消參賽資格。Selection rule: done a little > thought it through. Miss this page or fake it after the fact, and you're disqualified.
ROUND 2 — 複賽FINAL ROUND
線上 Live PitchOnline live pitch
  • 簡報不限頁數，但嚴格控時。No page limit, but strict timing.
  • 每隊 pitch 5 分鐘 ＋ 評審 Q&A 3–5 分鐘。Each team pitches 5 minutes + 3–5 minutes of judge Q&A.
  • Q&A 刻意往個人深挖，不做商業拷問：why you · why this · why now。Q&A deliberately digs into the personal, not a business grilling: why you · why this · why now.
Q1
你什麼時候第一次在意這件事？When did you first start caring about this?
Q2
做這個，你放棄了什麼？What did you give up to do this?
Q3
如果永遠賺不到錢，你還會做嗎？If it never made money, would you still do it?
